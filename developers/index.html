Reef for developers

A free, read-only API for Reef's BDAG pools on BlockDAG chain 1404, and a one-line widget you can put on your own site. Every number is read from the chain and cached for 60 seconds. No key, no sign-up, no tracking.

API

EndpointReturns
GET /api/v1/pairsEvery BDAG pool on Reef: token, symbol, reserves, pool price in BDAG, share of LP locked or burned.
GET /api/v1/pairs/<id>One pool, plus 24h volume and number of swaps. <id> is a token address, a pool address, or reef, nocap, cooked.
curl https://reefdex.fyi/api/v1/pairs/reef

priceBDAG is the rate in that pool's reserves right now, not a market price feed; a real swap gets a worse rate as it gets larger. Amounts are decimal strings so they don't lose precision. Responses allow any origin (CORS), so you can call them from a browser. Please cache on your side too and don't poll more than once a minute.

Pool widget

Paste this where you want the card. Change t= to your token's address; add &theme=light for light pages.

Swap links

Link straight to a pre-filled swap: https://reefdex.fyi/?from=bdag&to=<token address>&amount=1000. It only fills in the form; the user checks and signs in their own wallet.
