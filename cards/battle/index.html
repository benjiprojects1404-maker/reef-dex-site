Rules

  1. You and the house each get five cards. One card each is turned over per round.
  2. On odd rounds you pick the stat; on even rounds the house picks its best one.
  3. Both cards compare the same stat slot. Higher number wins the round; equal is a draw.
  4. Practice deals from all 150 cards. Play my cards uses the cards your wallet holds (after the reveal), topped up with random cards if you hold fewer than five.
  5. Nothing is staked and nothing is won. It's just for fun.
