How a mint works

All in one transaction; if any step fails, nothing happens.

  1. You pick COOKED or NOCAP and pay 100,000 BDAG.
  2. The contract swaps just enough of it for the token so the rest pairs exactly, and stops if the pool moved more than 2% from your quote.
  3. It adds both sides to that pool on Reef and sends the LP tokens to the dead address, so the liquidity can never be removed.
  4. It mints you the next card. All cards show a card back until the reveal.
  5. At sell-out (or after 30 days), anyone can trigger the reveal. A block hash nobody could know in advance picks the starting point in the card list, whose fingerprint was fixed before the sale: 0xe88c5841430e34ab12dc5c68af11614b8b894a5b49a35197159e95f1dea05c69

The contract has no withdraw function. Source and tests: GitHub.

Risks and disclosures

Unaudited. The card contract has tests but no independent audit.

Price effects. Each mint buys some COOKED or NOCAP before adding liquidity. The NOCAP pool is small (about 10,000 BDAG), so the first NOCAP mint moves its price sharply; later mints much less. This is pool maths, not a promise of any price.

Disclosure. Reef's developer holds NOCAP and COOKED liquidity and tokens, which these mints affect.
