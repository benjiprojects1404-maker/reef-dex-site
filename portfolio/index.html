Liquidity positions

PoolLP tokens and shareYour part of the pool

LP tokens (REEF-LP, 18 decimals) are what you receive when you add liquidity, and what you hand back to withdraw it. Each pool has its own LP token contract: the address under the pool name. "Add to wallet" shows that balance in MetaMask and similar wallets. Only LP tokens held by this address count here: liquidity locked in a lock contract belongs to the lock, so it shows under the lock's address instead.
