How it works

  1. Buy with BDAG during the window. You receive nothing yet — no presale REEF circulates before the pool exists.
  2. Anyone launches the pool by calling finalize() once the sale ends or sells out. The contract pairs every BDAG raised with REEF at exactly 225 BDAG each and sends unsold presale REEF to the dead address.
  3. Claim your REEF from the presale contract. You get what you paid ÷ 150.
  4. LP stays locked in the presale contract for 365 days, then can only go to Reef's liquidity wallet. Check it any time with lpUnlockTime().
  5. If the pool can't be launched within 7 days after the close, everyone can refund() their BDAG in full, and the launch is permanently cancelled.
Read this before buying. The pool opening at 225 is not a promise that REEF will trade at 225 or anywhere near 150. It opens thin, and the first people who sell will move the price, possibly a long way below what you paid. There is no established outside market for BDAG on this chain, so there is no dollar value here either. Only use BDAG you are fully prepared to lose. The contracts are not independently audited.
Where to buy: only through the presale contract address listed under Contracts on this page, once it is posted. It is not deployed yet. Anyone sending you a different address, a DM, or a “whitelist” link is not us.

FAQ

Why is there no dollar price anywhere?

The community chain's BDAG isn't listed on any outside exchange, so it has no established market price. Anything written in dollars would be made up. Every figure here is in BDAG on purpose. Don't confuse it with the BDAG token that BlockDAG Network lists on exchanges — that is on a different network.

Which chain is this? I've seen two.

Chain ID 1404 is currently used by two separate networks with different histories. $REEF and Reef live on the community-run network (rpc.blockdag.engineering, explorer.blockdag.engineering). Reef's own site has an RPC status panel that cross-checks endpoints. If your wallet shows something different from this page, stop and check before signing.

Does REEF pay passive income?

No. Holding REEF pays nothing: no staking yield, no share of Reef's fees, no buybacks. REEF can't mint new tokens, so any “yield” would only be the project handing out its own supply, and we'd rather not pretend that's income.

The income that does exist on Reef comes from providing liquidity. Anyone who adds REEF and BDAG to the REEF/BDAG pool earns 0.25% of every trade through it, in proportion to their share, paid by the traders, not by the project. That is market income, it goes up and down with trading, and liquidity providers carry real risks (including impermanent loss if the price moves). A time-limited extra reward for REEF/BDAG liquidity providers is being considered; nothing is promised, and it would be announced here first if it happens.

So what is REEF for?

Today: thanking early users and testers (the airdrop), and giving Reef its own pool. Planned, not promised: REEF as a bond across Reef, Nodal and Handshake — for example, a pool creator locking REEF for a “bonded” badge that is burned automatically if they pull the liquidity soon after launch. You put REEF at risk to be trusted; you don't get paid for holding it. Each of these would be a separate contract, published and tested before it goes live, and announced here.

Does buying at 150 and a pool opening at 225 mean a profit?

No. The 225 is only the ratio the pool starts at. The pool is small, so the first sales will push the price down, and there may be many sellers. You could end up with REEF worth less than you paid, or very little.

What if the presale doesn't sell out?

The pool still opens at exactly 225 BDAG per REEF, just with less depth: it pairs whatever was raised. Every unsold presale REEF goes to the dead address, so an under-filled sale can't benefit the team.

What stops the team from taking the BDAG?

The presale contract has no owner and no withdraw function for BDAG. The only ways BDAG leaves it are into the pool (finalize()) or back to buyers (refund()). The LP tokens stay inside the same contract for 365 days.

What happens if the pool can't be launched?

If finalize() hasn't succeeded within 7 days after the sale closes (after Fri Nov 13, 2026, 09:00 UTC+7), every buyer can call refund() and get their BDAG back in full. After the first refund, the launch is permanently cancelled, and the presale REEF stays locked in the contract forever.

Has this been audited?

No independent audit. The contracts were tested against Reef's real contracts with the real sale dates, including deliberate attempts to break them, and those tests will be published with the source. That is not the same as an audit. Treat everything here as experimental.

Could the ticker or name change?

The community chain's own ticker might change one day. That would be display-only: nothing in these contracts depends on the name “BDAG”.

Who can't take part?

US and UK persons may not buy in the presale or claim the airdrop. Only take part where it is lawful for you, and only if you are 18 or older.
